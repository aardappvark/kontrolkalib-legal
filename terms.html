3. Description of the Service

KontrolKalib is a controller diagnostic and calibration utility. It lets you test game controllers, measure analog-stick drift, and — for supported controllers connected over USB — re-centre stored calibration values. Some features are free and some may require a payment as described in Section 6.

Calibration re-centres a controller's stored calibration to compensate for wear. It does not repair hardware. See the Disclaimer & Risk Disclosure for the full risk disclosure, including the risk associated with writing to controller memory.
