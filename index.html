KontrolKalib

Built for Solana Seeker

Test any controller. Re-centre your PlayStation pad over USB. Prove it's fixed.

KontrolKalib turns your Solana Seeker into a precision instrument for game controllers. Connect almost any pad over Bluetooth to see its analog sticks live on a phosphor scope, measure resting drift, and check stick range and circularity. Plug a DualShock™ 4 or DualSense™ in over USB-C and KontrolKalib can re-centre its stored calibration — then run a before/after test so you can see the difference for yourself. Everything runs on-device: no accounts, no servers, no personal data collection.

KontrolKalib re-centres a controller's stored calibration to compensate for wear. It cannot repair worn potentiometers or other hardware, and it is not a financial service.
Calibration writes to your controller's own memory. It carries a small but real risk of leaving a controller in an unexpected state. Only genuine Sony controllers are calibrated, writes stop at the first unexpected response, and you use the calibration feature at your own risk. See the Disclaimer & Risk Disclosure.

Features

Universal Controller Tester

Connect almost any Bluetooth or USB gamepad and watch its sticks, triggers, buttons and D-pad respond live. Works with pads KontrolKalib cannot calibrate, too.

Drift Score & Phosphor Scope

Measure how far each stick rests from true centre and how much it wanders, on an oscilloscope-style display with a clear OK / Minor / Significant / Severe verdict.

PlayStation Calibration over USB

Re-centre a DualShock™ 4 (v1/v2) or DualSense™ over USB-C. A guided, step-locked wizard walks you through centre and range calibration.

Test Before You Keep

Calibration is applied temporarily first, so you can try it in a game before choosing to save it permanently. Honest, reversible, no surprises.

Safety-Gated Writes

Genuine-hardware and firmware checks run before any write, clones are refused, and a charge check guards permanent saves. Nothing is written after an unexpected response.

Runs On-Device

No accounts, no advertising, no third-party trackers. Controller details are read locally and never leave your phone. The only telemetry is an optional anonymous usage counter (aggregate counts only) that you can turn off in Settings. A wallet connection is used only for optional paid features.
