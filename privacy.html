Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026  |  Last updated: 7 August 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the KontrolKalib application ("App") handles information. It applies to the App and this website. We have designed KontrolKalib to collect as little information as possible.

KontrolKalib collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Your controller data never leaves your device. The App contains an optional anonymous usage counter — currently inactive in this build (see section 9) — which even when active would send only an event name with no identity attached, and which you can turn off in the App's Settings.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the App is AardAppvark, Melbourne, Victoria, Commonwealth of Australia. Contact: aardappvark@proton.me.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit to ourselves or any third party:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Your location, IP address, or device identifiers
  • Identifiable analytics or behavioural data (the only telemetry the App contains is the anonymous, aggregate usage counter described in section 9 — currently inactive in this build)
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Controller serial numbers, firmware details, or any controller data read during testing or calibration

5. Wallet Connection and Payments

Optional paid features require you to connect a Solana wallet using the Mobile Wallet Adapter and to authorise a payment from your own wallet. Where you use these features:

  • The App may read your wallet's public address to verify a payment or an entitlement. A public wallet address is, by its nature, public information on the blockchain.
  • We never access, request, or store your private keys or seed phrase. Signing happens inside your own wallet app.
  • Payment transactions are broadcast to and recorded on the public Solana blockchain, which is operated by third parties and is outside our control. We do not operate that network and cannot alter or delete on-chain records.

6. Network Requests

The core tester and calibration features function offline — no controller data is ever transmitted. The only data the App sends to us is the anonymous usage events described in section 9. Other network activity is limited to what is required to connect your wallet and submit a payment transaction you have authorised, which is handled by your wallet app and the Solana network, not by servers we operate.

To verify Seeker Genesis Token status, submit payments, and restore purchases, the App sends your wallet's public address to Solana RPC providers: api.mainnet-beta.solana.com and, as a fallback, solana-rpc.publicnode.com (Allnodes). These providers receive your IP address and the public address. Private keys never leave your wallet.

7. No Accounts

KontrolKalib has no user accounts, registration, log-in, or authentication other than the optional wallet connection described above, which does not create an account with us.

9. Anonymous Usage Analytics

Status: currently inactive in this build. The current build of the App ships without an analytics key and the analytics service has not been deployed, so no analytics events are sent and the public stats page is not yet live. The description below applies only if a future update activates the feature; this policy will be kept current if that happens.

If activated: to understand which features people use and where the App is popular, the App sends short, anonymous event pings to an analytics endpoint we operate (a Cloudflare Worker with its own database). Each ping contains only an event-type label — for example app_open, calibrate_saved_permanent, or geo_blocked. It carries no account, no device identifier, no wallet address, and no controller data.

The country and city are derived at the network edge from the connection and are retained only as aggregate counts (for example "5 calibrations saved in Australia today"). Your IP address is never logged or stored by us. We keep only these aggregate totals — never a per-user or per-device record — so the data cannot be traced back to an individual.

This is first-party only: the App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not build profiles about you and do not engage in automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning you. Because the data is anonymous and aggregate it does not identify you (see GDPR Recital 26). You can disable it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous usage stats. Once the service is active, the live aggregate totals will be public at kontrolkalib-analytics.aardappvark.workers.dev/public-stats.

10. Children's Privacy

KontrolKalib is intended for users aged 18 or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Users

Because the App does not transmit personal data to us, there is no international transfer of your personal data by us. Blockchain transactions you authorise are processed on a globally distributed public network operated by third parties.

12. Data Security

Information processed by the App remains within your device's application sandbox, protected by the security model of the Android operating system. Because we hold no personal data on servers, there is no server-side store of your data to be breached.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA, and Singapore's PDPA. These include rights to access, correction, erasure, and portability of personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, we generally have no personal data to provide, correct, or delete. Any data the App stores locally can be removed by you at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

14. Data Retention and Android Auto Backup

We retain no personal data. Data stored locally by the App (settings, history) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the App's settings and progress data are included in your device's encrypted Google backup, survive uninstalling the App, and may be restored when you reinstall. Wallet data is always excluded from backup. You can exclude the App from backup in Android Settings or delete the backup from your Google account.

15. Do Not Track

The App does not track users across applications or websites, so "Do Not Track" signals are not applicable.
