Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026  |  Last updated: 12 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the KontrolKalib application ("App") handles information. It applies to the App and this website. We have designed KontrolKalib to collect as little information as possible.

KontrolKalib collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no servers operated by us, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything the App does happens locally on your device.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for the App is MidMightBit Games, Melbourne, Victoria, Commonwealth of Australia. Contact: aardappvark@proton.me.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or transmit to ourselves or any third party:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
  • Your location, IP address, or device identifiers
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or behavioural data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Controller serial numbers, firmware details, or any controller data read during testing or calibration

4. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To do its job, the App reads and processes certain information only on your device. This information is not transmitted to us and is not shared with any third party by us:

5. Wallet Connection and Payments

Optional paid features require you to connect a Solana wallet using the Mobile Wallet Adapter and to authorise a payment from your own wallet. Where you use these features:

  • The App may read your wallet's public address to verify a payment or an entitlement. A public wallet address is, by its nature, public information on the blockchain.
  • We never access, request, or store your private keys or seed phrase. Signing happens inside your own wallet app.
  • Payment transactions are broadcast to and recorded on the public Solana blockchain, which is operated by third parties and is outside our control. We do not operate that network and cannot alter or delete on-chain records.

6. Network Requests

The core tester and calibration features function offline and make no network requests to us. The App has no analytics or advertising network calls. Network activity is limited to what is required to connect your wallet and submit a payment transaction you have authorised, which is handled by your wallet app and the Solana network, not by servers we operate.

7. No Accounts

KontrolKalib has no user accounts, registration, log-in, or authentication other than the optional wallet connection described above, which does not create an account with us.

9. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not build profiles about you and do not engage in automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

10. Children's Privacy

KontrolKalib is intended for users aged 18 or older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International Users

Because the App does not transmit personal data to us, there is no international transfer of your personal data by us. Blockchain transactions you authorise are processed on a globally distributed public network operated by third parties.

12. Data Security

Information processed by the App remains within your device's application sandbox, protected by the security model of the Android operating system. Because we hold no personal data on servers, there is no server-side store of your data to be breached.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), Brazil's LGPD, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA, and Singapore's PDPA. These include rights to access, correction, erasure, and portability of personal data. Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, we generally have no personal data to provide, correct, or delete. Any data the App stores locally can be removed by you at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

14. Data Retention

We retain no personal data. Data stored locally by the App (settings, history, profiles) remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

15. Do Not Track

The App does not track users across applications or websites, so "Do Not Track" signals are not applicable.
