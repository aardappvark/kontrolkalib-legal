4. User Representations and Warranties

By downloading or using KontrolKalib, you represent and warrant that you are not located in, ordinarily resident in, or a national of any restricted jurisdiction listed above, and that you are not on any applicable sanctions or denied-persons list.

5. How Screening Is Applied

Consistent with our privacy-preserving design, KontrolKalib does not track your precise location or transmit location data to us. Screening is applied app-wide, at launch: the App checks privacy-preserving signals available on the device — the SIM country, the network country, and the device locale — and if any of them resolves to a restricted jurisdiction the entire App is blocked, including the free tester and calibration features, and a notice naming this policy is shown instead. These signals are indicative only and are not a substitute for your own compliance with applicable law.

Note: screening applies to the whole App, not only to paid features. Regardless of any technical screening, your use of the App from a restricted jurisdiction is prohibited.

6. Consequences of Violation

If we determine that you have used the App in violation of applicable sanctions, we may restrict or terminate your access. You remain solely responsible for ensuring your own use of the App complies with all laws applicable to you.

7. Updates

Sanctions regimes change frequently. We may update this list at any time. The restricted jurisdictions above reflect our understanding as at the "Last updated" date and are not an exhaustive statement of all applicable restrictions.
